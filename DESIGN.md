# Portfolio visual direction

Direction comes from the user's supplied portfolio reference on September 16, 2026. The reference replaces the previous fullscreen scroll journey.

- Identity: a personal portfolio in a rounded macOS-inspired window, with a personal profile photo in the right side of the hero.
- Personality: quiet, straightforward, spacious, and personal.
- Palette: warm gray outer canvas, white window, olive-charcoal text, pale blue hero and cafe preview, neutral project and Threads placeholders. Dark mode preserves these surface relationships.
- Typography: Satoshi adds a warm geometric voice that suits the personal, macOS-inspired frame without disturbing its quiet hierarchy. Headings use medium weight and restrained size; body copy remains readable on mobile, with Arial/Helvetica as fallbacks.
- Composition: macOS traffic lights and compact navigation; two-column introduction; three project previews; three tall Threads slots; centered tech stack heading and grouped skills; a minimal footer.
- Whitespace: generous separation between sections, especially before the tech stack, follows the reference's reading rhythm.
- Shape: rounded outer frame and hero, smaller radii on content previews, pill-shaped hero actions. Shadow only separates the outer frame and primary action from their surfaces.
- Icons: small send, document, theme and social symbols clarify actions. Traffic lights reproduce the user's explicit macOS reference, as a single home link rather than fake window controls.
- Motion: slow, staggered hero entrance; one-time project and tech-stack reveals on scroll; a smooth mobile-menu transition; restrained hover feedback; and smooth anchor scrolling.
- Content: supplied name, introduction, profile photo, project names and descriptions. Existing verified skills and contact destinations are retained. Screenshots, project URLs, and Threads posts remain clearly labeled placeholders until supplied.
- Dials: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.
