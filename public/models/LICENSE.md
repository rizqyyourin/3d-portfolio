The original ASSET.glb was supplied by the portfolio owner and is adapted as macbook.glb with embedded textures resized to 2048px and base color / packed material maps encoded as JPEG. Portfolio adaptations: normalized scene scale and placement, custom lighting, floating motion, and a separate interactive display overlay. Original geometry remains intact; the unmodified source is preserved in macbook/source/ASSET.glb.
